Polish

Light editing for your dictated text. Keep your meaning and your voice.

Text polish

  • Polish selected text

    Select text in an editor, then press the shortcut. Fixes grammar, sentence structure and accidental repetition while preserving ideas, emotions, voice and paragraph breaks. Raw and live dictation never run an extra polish call. Esc cancels. Review the result, then use Copy when ready. Your document is never changed automatically. Terminal targets are skipped.

  • Polish shortcut

    Choose Change, then press your shortcut. Default F8.

  • Uses the provider and key from AI answers. Your model choice stays separate.

Whisper on this computer

Fallback for the cloud engine

  • Each size shows its approximate GPU memory, including about 1 GB of CUDA runtime. Bigger models are more accurate and slower. Auto keeps tiny and base on the processor; cuda loads the model into GPU memory at startup and warms it.

Personal vocabulary

  • Exact spellings, separated by commas or new lines. Hints guide Gemini, Groq and local Whisper; recognition can still vary. Gemini uses up to 100 terms. Groq and Whisper use whole terms within 224 UTF-8 bytes, so put priority words first. OpenRouter speech does not use hints. Polish also receives these spellings.
