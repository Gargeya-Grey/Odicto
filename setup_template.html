Advanced (hotkeys)

The gearbox (top left) sets chord style: Long ride taps to start and tap to stop; Short ride holds while you speak. Live tap (F7) is always press once to start, press again to stop. While Odicto is running, F7 is captured so it does not fire in other apps.
